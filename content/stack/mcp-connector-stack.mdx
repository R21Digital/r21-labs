R21 runs agents against production systems, not sample data. This is the list of
what is actually connected, and the count on the homepage is this list's length
rather than a number someone typed.

## The three tiers

They are not interchangeable, and the difference is who is trusted with what:

- **Infrastructure R21 owns.** Automation and anything needing an external
  trigger runs on **[n8n](/tools/n8n), on R21's own server**. Hosting is **Vercel**, domains
  sit at **Cloudflare**, data on **Supabase**, mail through **AWS SES**.
- **Systems R21 operates on a client's behalf** — Stripe, Wix, Metricool,
  ClickUp, the Google surfaces. An agent reaching these is acting inside someone
  else's account, which is a different risk category from reading a repository.
- **Reference and capability** — [Context7](/tools/context7), Exa, NotebookLM,
  [Playwright](/tools/playwright), Hugging Face. These bring information or ability in rather than reaching out to
  a system of record.

## What "connected" is allowed to mean

Every entry here was observed connected in a live working session. That is a
deliberately weak claim, honestly stated: it means the connector authenticated
and answered, not that R21 uses all eighteen every week.

The temptation on a page like this is to list what is *installed*, which is
always a longer and more impressive list, and always partly fiction. The rule
this site is built on applies to its own stack page.

## Make.com is not on this list, and the reason is measured

R21 migrated its automations off Make to self-hosted n8n through August 2026 —
in stages rather than in one cutover. Eleven `LeadNotify` relays moved on
2026-08-06, taking active scenarios from 18 to 7; two more followed on 08-08.

The effect is the strongest number R21 has on the value of the move. Measured
over a clean four-day window entirely after the migration, consumption fell to
**8.5× below** the pre-migration rate.

**What is not claimed:** a date on which Make was switched off. The last
recorded reading, 2026-08-10, still showed five active scenarios, and no
measurement after that has been taken. An earlier version of this page named a
retirement date that nothing supports, and both R21's internal notes and this
entry had settled on different wrong dates — which is the ordinary way an
unrecorded fact becomes a confident one. It says what was measured, and stops.
