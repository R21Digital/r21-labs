Graphify-Labs' Graphify, which R21 adapted for Windows.

It turns a codebase and its docs, schemas and configs into a queryable knowledge graph using local deterministic AST parsing, with every edge explained and no vector store. R21 maintains the Windows adaptation; the skill in our workspace declares `graphify-windows`. The upstream tool is Graphify-Labs' work and the adaptation is ours, and both halves of that sentence matter.

Verified against the GitHub API on 2026-08-20: Apache-2.0, 108,622 stars. Two attributions that circulated internally were wrong and are worth naming, because the site exists to stop exactly this. It is not by "Chase AI" — that is the person whose video covered it, and our wiki's section heading turned coverage into authorship. And the "~60K stars" figure was accurate to the recording date, not to now; star counts are fetched or omitted here, never carried over from a note.
