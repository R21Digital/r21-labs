# Issue 1: the question, written before the report

**Question.** How many Puerto Rico business websites block AI crawlers?

**Claim tested.** "Since Cloudflare began blocking AI crawlers by default in 2025, businesses are shutting ChatGPT out." A claim we have heard, often tied to Cloudflare's 2025 change. This census tests it in robots.txt and in server responses to an identified visitor; blocking at Cloudflare's network is a separate mechanism it cannot see. Cloudflare's own announcement of the default is dated July 1, 2025: https://www.cloudflare.com/press/press-releases/2025/cloudflare-just-changed-how-ai-crawlers-scrape-the-internet-at-large/

**Frame that answers it.** Business websites listed in OpenStreetMap (via Overpass, data as of 2026-10-22T20:19Z) and on the Puerto Rico Chamber of Commerce public "New Members" page (list dated 2026-05-27). 150 drawn at random from each source, 300 in all; 29 removed after the draw as not local; 246 of the rest answered on 2026-10-22. Each site's robots.txt read for eight AI crawlers, and the server's answer to an openly identified research visitor recorded.
